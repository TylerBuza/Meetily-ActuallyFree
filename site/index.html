The workspace

From the call to the follow-up.

01 / Capture

Record both sides

Control your mic and computer audio separately, or import an existing recording. Transcribe live with Parakeet or Whisper and add vocabulary hints for names and project terms.

02 / Review

Find the speaker

Click a timestamp to hear the original audio. Rename speakers, merge duplicate labels, or refine them after the call. Optional voice profiles suggest familiar names for you to review.

03 / Follow up

Keep the decisions

Write notes beside the transcript, generate a summary from a template, and give tasks owners and due dates. Search across meetings or export to Markdown, Word, PDF, text, or JSON.

Before you start

Good to know.

Can I use Meetily offline?

Yes. Download a transcription model and, if you want AI notes, a local summary model during setup. After that, both can run offline. Choosing cloud transcription sends audio to your provider; cloud summaries send meeting text.

Does a bot have to join my call?

No. The desktop app records the microphone and computer audio you select. Windows can capture multiple selected apps; macOS supports one running app at a time. On Linux, choose an output or follow the system default.

How does it know who spoke?

The microphone track is labeled “You.” On-device diarization groups remote speech by voice, not by identity. You can assign names yourself. Remembering a voice across meetings is an opt-in beta feature.

What does “Actually Free” mean?

This is an independent, MIT-licensed fork of Meetily by Zackriya Solutions. The app has no paid tier. Local models need no API key; optional external services have their own accounts, prices, and privacy terms.
